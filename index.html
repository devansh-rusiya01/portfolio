Interfaces that

I'm a computer science engineer focused on full-stack product work. Most of what I build lives in the JavaScript ecosystem — React on the front, Node and Express behind it, MongoDB or MySQL underneath.

I care about the parts most people skip: sensible data models, predictable state, and interfaces that load instantly without needing a manual. I've shipped an EdTech platform, a hiring portal, and a handful of smaller tools that do one thing well.

Away from the keyboard I'm usually working through data structures and algorithms, reading about design systems, or pulling apart developer tooling to see how it's made.
